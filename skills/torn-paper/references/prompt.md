# Prompt skeleton

Send the photograph as a semantic reference. Write one prompt per poster. Fill the blanks from the photograph. Delete any sentence that does not apply.

```text
Semantic reference only. Rebuild the scene as layered cut paper. No photographic pixels, no pasted photo texture, no second scene.

Canvas: [actual photo aspect ratio after EXIF, for example 3:2, 2:3, 3:4, 4:3, 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1], full bleed, flat scan. Produce ONE complete artwork, not a grid. No mockup, no curled corner, no floor shadow.

COLOR LOCK, taken from this photograph: [five to eight colors, named plainly, such as "vivid blue sky, white clouds, deep green leaves, small orange blossoms, white shirt, black strap"]. Added cream is limited to narrow torn fibers; original pale walls, petals, clothing or sky keep their actual colors. Do not recolor the scene to a generic beige, brown, or gray palette. Preserve the source light-dark balance.

SCALE: the [hero] stays large, in the same place in the frame as the photograph. A person who knows the photograph can still name it. Keep [two relationships, such as "the wheel behind the roof" or "the person in front of the tree"].

TEAR EVENT: [one event from tear-events.md, in physical words]. The torn edge is irregular, warm, and fibrous, with a shallow notch and a few longer pulls. It is a narrow edge, not an outline sticker and not a deckled frame around the poster.

DOMINANT EDGE: [the one specific sheet boundary where the main tear must be visible]. Keep [other main subject boundaries] as clean cut-paper edges without cream outlines. Do not add unrelated rips to the sky or walls to simulate variety.

SHEETS: [name 3 to 5 sheets and what each one is]. Soft contact shadow only where one sheet overlaps another.

KEEP: [the specific parts that make it this place: rose window, couplet, cap, sunglasses, arch, cabin row].
OMIT: extra people, extra buildings, invented words, logos, watermarks.

Hard avoids: a small icon on an empty sky, a black silhouette logo, a tear across a face or a shirt, flowers stuck on skin, photographic skin, a different face, readable nonsense text.
```

If the hero is a person, add:

```text
The person is one intact sheet. No tear crosses the face, neck, hair, hat, glasses, shirt, arms, hands, or strap. The tear only separates that sheet from the background.
```

If the building carries real lettering and the letters are certain, add the exact words and say to omit every other inscription. If the letters are uncertain, say to omit all lettering.
